# Inamdar Business Analysis — Brand Guide

## 1. Brand Positioning

**What we are:** A risk-intelligence product that produces source-backed,
public-records based business risk reports on Indian companies, vendors,
promoters and counterparties.

**Who we serve:** Founders, investors, procurement and vendor-onboarding teams,
lenders and fintechs, exporters and distributors, and professional advisers
(CAs, CSs, lawyers, consultants).

**The job we do:** We help people understand *who they are dealing with* before
they sign, invest, lend, or onboard — by compiling and citing risk indicators
that already exist in official and credible public records.

**Positioning statement:**
> Inamdar Business Analysis is a counterparty due-diligence product. We turn
> scattered public records into a clear, source-backed risk picture, so a
> business decision is made with eyes open rather than on trust alone.

**What we are NOT:** We are not a law firm, a credit-rating agency, a private
investigator, or a certification body. We do not give legal advice, issue
opinions, or guarantee outcomes. Our brand voice must never drift toward
law-firm advertising or sensational fraud-detection claims.

**Category cues we want to evoke:** business intelligence, risk analytics,
data verification, compliance tooling — *not* litigation, courts, or advocacy.

## 2. Logo Concept

The mark is a **shield that doubles as a stack of records.**

- **Shield silhouette** → verification, trust, due diligence.
- **Three ascending bars inside** → structured data, records, business
  intelligence (a quiet nod to analytics, never a literal chart).
- **One highlighted gold node** above the tallest bar → a surfaced *risk
  signal* / indicator — the single thing our work exists to reveal.
- **Layered reading** → the shield outline also scans as stacked documents /
  records being protected and examined.

**Deliberately avoided:** scales of justice, gavels, courthouse columns,
magnifying-glass-on-paper clichés rendered literally, or anything that reads as
"law firm". The mark should feel like a SaaS risk-intelligence product.

**Wordmark:** "Inamdar Business Analysis" set in Sora (display), with an
optional uppercase "Risk Intelligence" descriptor in tracked-out caps.

**Construction & clear space:** Keep clear space around the mark equal to the
height of the gold node. Minimum mark size: 24px. Never recolor the node to
anything but gold — it is the brand's focal accent.

## 3. Color Palette

A deep navy/slate base lets the accent colors act like signal lights.

| Token            | Hex       | Role |
|------------------|-----------|------|
| Ink 950          | `#05080f` | Page background (deepest) |
| Ink 900          | `#0a0f1c` | Section background |
| Ink 800          | `#0f1626` | Card / surface base |
| Ink 700          | `#16203a` | Raised surface, borders |
| Ink 600          | `#1e2b4a` | Hairlines, scrollbar |
| Emerald          | `#10b981` | Primary accent — "verified / clear" |
| Emerald Soft     | `#34d399` | Emerald highlights |
| Cyan             | `#22d3ee` | Secondary accent — "intelligence / scan" |
| Cyan Soft        | `#67e8f9` | Cyan highlights, eyebrow text |
| Gold             | `#d4a13c` | Focal accent — "signal / attention" |
| Gold Soft        | `#e8c477` | Gold highlights |
| Slate 200        | `#e2e8f0` | Body text |
| Slate 400        | `#94a3b8` | Muted text |

**Usage rules**
- Gold is rationed. It marks the *one* thing that needs attention (the signal
  node, a flagged risk, a key CTA accent). Overusing gold kills its meaning.
- Emerald = good / clear / verified. Cyan = active / scanning / data.
- Risk levels: Low → emerald, Moderate → gold, Elevated/High → a warm
  amber-red (`#f59e0b` / `#f87171`) used sparingly and never sensationally.

## 4. Typography

| Use            | Typeface | Notes |
|----------------|----------|-------|
| Display / Headlines | **Sora** | Geometric, confident, modern. Used for H1–H3 and the wordmark. |
| Body / UI      | **Inter** | Highly legible at small sizes; tables, labels, paragraphs. |

**Scale (fluid, Tailwind):**
- Hero H1: `text-4xl → text-6xl`, `font-display`, tight tracking.
- Section H2: `text-3xl → text-4xl`, `font-display`.
- Card H3: `text-lg → text-xl`, `font-display` / semibold.
- Body: `text-base`, `leading-relaxed`, slate-300/400.
- Eyebrow / labels: `text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.2em]`, cyan-soft.

**Rules:** Headlines in Sora; never set long body copy in Sora. Keep line length
to ~70 characters. Use tracked-out caps only for short labels.

## 5. Tone of Voice

**Calm, precise, and useful.** We are the steady analyst in the room, not a
salesperson and not an alarmist.

- **Plain over dramatic.** "Litigation search returned 3 matched records as of
  the date of search" — not "We caught them red-handed."
- **Sourced, not asserted.** Every finding points to where it came from and when
  it was checked.
- **Hedged honestly.** We describe *indicators* and *available records*, and we
  state the date of search. We never imply certainty we don't have.
- **Respectful of the subject.** Reports are neutral. We do not editorialize
  about people or companies.

## 6. Do / Don't Language

### ✅ Do say
- public-records based
- source-backed findings
- risk indicators
- date of search
- identity match
- counterparty due diligence
- available public records
- as of the date of search
- record matched / no record matched in the sources searched

### ❌ Don't say
- "legal advice" / "legal opinion"
- "expert advocate" / "best lawyer" / "top law firm"
- "guaranteed fraud detection" / "we guarantee…"
- "certificate of good standing"
- "credit rating" / "credit score"
- "private investigation" / "we investigate"
- sensational, defamatory or fear-mongering claims about any party
- absolute promises about future conduct ("they will never default")

### Standard disclaimer (use verbatim)
> This report is based on public records, information provided by the client and
> records available from official or credible sources as of the date of search.
> It is not a credit rating, legal opinion, private investigation, certificate
> of good standing or guarantee of future conduct.
